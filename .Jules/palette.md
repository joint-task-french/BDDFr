## 2024-05-18 - Missing ARIA Labels on Close Buttons\n**Learning:** Icon-only close and remove buttons (using "✕") throughout the application lacked ARIA labels, rendering them inaccessible to screen reader users.\n**Action:** Ensure all icon-only buttons include descriptive `aria-label` attributes in French (e.g., `aria-label="Fermer"` or `aria-label="Retirer l'attribut"`) to comply with accessibility standards.
